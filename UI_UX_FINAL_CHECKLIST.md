# GreenLeaf BD UI/UX Final Target

- Bottom navigation: Home | Shop | My Account only.
- Large, readable typography; body text >= 15px.
- Category controls have large touch targets (>= 42px).
- Product cards use consistent image ratios and spacing.
- Plant cards expose Add to Wishlist clearly.
- Cart uses +/- quantity controls and updates totals.
- Checkout collects delivery information before Place Order.
- Plant Care has DOs & DON'Ts.
- Gemini AI is a floating assistant and does not replace bottom navigation.
- Shop categories: Plants, Seeds, Pots, Planters, Plant Care.
- Plant categories are filtered correctly.
- Account has a visible Back control and Logout.
- Avoid tiny text, crowded rows, hidden buttons, and overlapping bottom navigation.
